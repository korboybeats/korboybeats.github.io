ipadecrypt

Decrypt App Store apps directly on your jailbroken iPhone.

Features

  • Browse and search installed apps
  • Decrypt the currently installed version
  • Download and decrypt the latest App Store version
  • Download and decrypt the latest version compatible with your iOS
  • Select, install, or decrypt an exact historical App Store version
  • Install a selected version without decrypting it
  • View live progress and copy logs
  • Share completed IPA files
  • Open the decrypted output folder in Filza
  • View and refresh Apple ID authentication status

Compatibility

  • Rootless and RootHide jailbreaks
  • iOS 14 through iOS 17
  • Requires AppSync Unified and appinst
  • Some newer SC_Info formats and certain arm64e apps may not decrypt
